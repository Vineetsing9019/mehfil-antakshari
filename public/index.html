About Mehfil

Mehfil is a free online Antakshari game. Start a table, share the link on WhatsApp, and take turns singing Hindi film songs. Each singer starts with the letter the last singer ended on, and the table votes on every song. No friends online? Play with AI singers. Or open a Sing together table for up to 8 people. Works in any browser.
