    How to play

    1. Share your table link on WhatsApp, or start a table and invite a singer.
    2. The host starts the game. One singer at a time, like carrom.
    3. Sing a song that starts with the letter in the middle, then type its last word.
    4. The table votes 👍 or 👎 on your song. Passing or a rejected song costs a ❤.
    5. Last singer standing wins, or the stronger team.
