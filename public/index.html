About Mehfil

Mehfil: play Antakshari online

Mehfil is a free online Antakshari game for friends and families. Start a table, share the link on WhatsApp, and take turns singing Hindi film songs. Each singer starts with the letter the last singer ended on, and the table votes on every song.

No friends online? Play with AI singers, who sing original Mehfil songs out loud. You can also open a Sing together table for up to 8 people, with a song queue, chat and emoji reactions. Mehfil works in any browser, and you can add it to your phone's home screen.

For ages 13 and up. Your name is shown to other singers. Privacy
