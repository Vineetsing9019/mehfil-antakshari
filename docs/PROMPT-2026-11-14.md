# Mehfil: working prompt (14 Nov 2026)

Written for: whoever builds or reviews Mehfil next, including Claude.

## Product
Mehfil is a free Hindi-film antakshari game. Friends join by WhatsApp link, sing in turns or sing together like a call, vote on songs, chat and react. It should feel simple, premium and safe for 30–50k users.

## Rules for the work
1. Keep the UI simple, like Instagram or WhatsApp. One primary button per screen, with secondary and tertiary buttons for everything else.
2. Start game sits at the top. Leave is always visible, including during a round.
3. Settings, chat and reactions are small controls that open small panels.
4. Every table is a normal call: everyone's mic on, everyone hears everyone.
5. Only use songs we are allowed to use: original songs written for Mehfil, or songs with a free licence shown with credit. Never copy commercial recordings or make AI copies of real singers' voices.
6. Never push to, or deploy from, any account other than the Mehfil owner's. No Storm, T3, Supabase or Vercel accounts. Deploys are done by the owner on Render.
7. Test every change before reporting it done, and show the proof.
