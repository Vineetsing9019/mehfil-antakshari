# What's new in Mehfil

Newest first. Every change is also in the [commit history](https://github.com/Vineetsing9019/mehfil-antakshari/commits/main).

## 15 November 2026 (evening)

- **Find a friend** on home: type a name or @username to see who is online, then Invite them, or Join them if they are at an open table.
- Privacy fix: the code of a link-only table is no longer sent to everyone on the home screen, so nobody can join it without the link.
- 80 server checks and 113 browser checks, all passing.

## 15 November 2026

**Looks and feel**
- New Mehfil logo (म in the stage ring) for the app icon, browser tab, home-screen icon and link preview.
- First screen shows a 5-second demo of AI singers passing the letter along, so the game explains itself.
- Screens cross-fade into each other.
- Home fits on one phone screen: one big "Play now", two smaller choices, one row of singers.
- After a game, Share makes a picture of the result (winner, scores, songs) for WhatsApp status or Instagram.

**Playing**
- Play now: a game with 2 AI singers that starts at once. Daily challenge: one letter a day for everyone.
- Your turn: type only the song. The next letter comes from its last word, or pick any letter A to Z. Pass sits next to Done.
- Say the song name with the 🎤 button instead of typing (Chrome, Edge, Android).
- Title suggestions after 2 letters (they help with spelling, not with the answer).
- "+15 ⚡ fast" points pop-up, badge pop-ups with confetti, a tick in the last 5 seconds.
- Hindi and Marathi tables show the Devanagari letter big. The vote question follows the theme (90s, actor, tune).
- Songs sung are listed at Game over; non-hosts can ask for a rematch.

**AI singers**
- 20 AI singers, each with its own voice setting; every one is clearly marked AI.
- 101 original Mehfil songs (Hindi, Marathi, English), each with a verse and a chorus. Lyrics light up line by line, karaoke style.
- Soft tanpura under their voice; the host can Skip; Easy / Normal / Hard levels; they cheer good songs in chat.
- Leaving a table stops their voice at once.

**Video calling**
- Turn your camera on at a link-only table and see each other above the stage. Cameras are off by default, never shown at open tables (strangers), and at most 4 at a table. Making a table public turns every camera off.

**Friends and safety**
- Invite by name from inside a table, even mid-game (the friend listens and sings next round).
- Tables can be link-only. Mute one person, only for yourself. Tap a name in chat to block or report.
- Bad-word filter for chat, names, cities and bios (it leaves real names alone).
- Day streaks, a crown for the week's top singer, QR code to join in the same room.

**Speed and reliability**
- First screen in about 0.8 s on a first visit and 0.4 s after that (simulated 4G, phone-speed processor).
- Files are compressed, fonts don't hold up the page, and the app opens from a saved copy on later visits.
- Works offline: the app opens and says plainly that it will reconnect.
- 78 server checks and 107 browser checks (including a two-person video call), all passing.

## 14 November 2026
